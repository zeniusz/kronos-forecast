Forecast settings

More futures give a steadier average but take longer. Variety sets how much the simulated futures differ from each other. The Base model downloads about 400 MB the first time you use it.

Times are shown in your local time. Kronos reads past price and volume only. It knows nothing about news, and its futures are random samples, so treat any forecast as one informed guess. Not financial advice. Nothing here connects to an exchange or places a trade.
